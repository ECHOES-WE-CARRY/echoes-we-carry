    The House

    We carry the echoes our elders left behind.

    Three generations ago, our founders began collecting the silks, jewels and ritual objects that were disappearing from family chests across India — not to archive them, but to keep them alive. Every piece in our house is documented, restored and, wherever possible, re-woven by the same artisan families who first made it.

    Today the atelier works with forty-eight master hands across twelve regions — from the brocade looms of Banaras to the temple workshops of the South — translating old craft into heirlooms that belong to the future.

    “An heirloom is a conversation across time. We simply keep the line open.”

    — Amrita & Raghav Sen, custodians
